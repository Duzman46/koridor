Koridor Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Koridor is a strategy game that can be played offline and online.

Data collected and processed

When online play is selected, Firebase Authentication creates a random anonymous user identifier for the device. The room code, anonymous player identifiers, board state, move history, and timestamps are processed in Firebase Realtime Database to synchronize the match and validate room membership and turn order.

Game settings and statistics such as total games, wins, and losses are stored locally on the device using Android DataStore.

The free version may display Google AdMob ads. Google may process information such as the advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, and app/device diagnostics under its own policies. Where required, consent or privacy choices are requested through Google User Messaging Platform before an ad request. Users can reopen ad privacy options from Settings.

Premium “Remove Ads” is a one-time in-app product processed by Google Play. Koridor does not see or store payment card information. The app uses only the product identifier, purchase status, and purchase token to confirm ownership and disable ads.

Permissions

The app uses internet/network state for online matches, ads, and purchase verification, and vibration for game feedback. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permission.

Service providers

Koridor uses Google Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database, Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, and Google Play Billing. Data is processed under these providers’ terms for advertising, online gameplay, fraud prevention, security, and payment processing. We do not sell user data.

Retention and deletion

Users can clear Koridor’s local settings, statistics, and Premium cache from Android app settings. Purchase ownership remains associated with the Google Play account and can be verified again with “Restore Purchase.” Uninstalling the app removes local data. A waiting room is deleted when it is closed; completed or abandoned online room data may be removed during operational cleanup. Online deletion requests can be submitted to the project owner with the room code.

Children’s privacy

The app does not ask users for a name, email address, or phone number. Children should share online room codes only with people they know. Play Console audience and advertising settings must be configured for the intended age group before release.

Changes and contact

Updates will be published in this document and on the store listing. Before release, this policy will be published at a public HTTPS URL and the developer contact address will be added here.
